How it works.

Four steps from your checkout page to your customer's door. That is the whole handoff.

  1. Step 01

    You sell.

    A customer checks out on your store. Nothing about your storefront changes.

  2. Step 02

    We receive.

    Your inventory arrives at our dock. We count it in and put it away on our shelves.

  3. Step 03

    We pick and pack.

    We pull the order off the shelf, box it, and put the label on the outside.

  4. Step 04

    We ship.

    It leaves 1933 S. Broadway on a truck, headed to your customer.

What you get back.

Handing off the boxes is not a line item. It is space, hours, and headroom.

Your space back.

No more inventory stacked in the spare room, the garage, or the corner of the office.

Your hours back.

No more nights spent taping cartons and printing labels at the kitchen table.

Room to grow.

Add products and take more orders without adding shelves, space, or a lease of your own.
